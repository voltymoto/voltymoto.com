Why we build for you

A better working day, that happens to be electric.

Vietnam is moving its cities off petrol on a legislated clock, and commercial riders are named first in every rule. The riders with the least room in their budget are being asked to change first.

So we built the vehicle for the rider doing 200 km a day, and priced its running cost below the petrol bike it replaces.
