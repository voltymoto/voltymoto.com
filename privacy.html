Who is responsible for your data

NUEN MOTO Company Limited is the data controller for personal data collected through voltymoto.com.

NUEN MOTO Company Limited, No. 11, 10th Street, Sala, An Loi Dong, Thu Duc City, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Enterprise code 0317847638.

Contact for any privacy question or request: ride@nuenmoto.com, or write to the address above marked for the attention of the Data Protection contact.

We handle personal data in line with Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP, both in force since 1 January 2026, which replaced Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP.

What we collect

We keep this deliberately narrow. We collect only what we need to answer you and run the site.

We do not ask for, and we ask you not to send us, sensitive personal data as defined by the Law on Personal Data Protection. That includes health data, biometric data, political or religious views, and financial account details. We do not process payments on this website.

Why we are allowed to use it

  • Your consent. When you submit a form, you are consenting to us using those details to respond and to follow up about the Volty U1. You can withdraw that consent at any time.
  • Performing a contract or taking steps before one. Where you have asked us to prepare a quote, a pilot, or an agreement.
  • Our legitimate interest in a secure, working website. Server logs and basic analytics fall here, kept to what security and operations require.
  • Legal obligation. Where Vietnamese law requires us to keep or produce records.

Cookies and measurement

This site uses only what it needs to function and to give us a basic count of page visits. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising trackers on this site, and we do not build advertising profiles from your visit.

Your browser will accept a cookie or store a small amount of local data to remember choices such as your language selection. You can block or delete these through your browser settings. Blocking them may mean the site forgets your language preference, but the content stays readable.

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and video hosted by our provider. Those requests reveal your IP address to that provider as a technical necessity of delivering the file.

Who else sees it

We share personal data only where there is a reason to:

  • Service providers who host the site, deliver our email, or store our records, acting on our instructions and bound to protect the data.
  • Battery-swap or fleet partners, only where you have asked us to introduce you, and only with your consent at that time. We do not pass rider or fleet contact details to a partner as a matter of course.
  • Authorities, where Vietnamese law obliges us to disclose.
  • A buyer or successor, if the business or part of it is transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to data transferred.

We do not sell personal data. Under the Law on Personal Data Protection, buying and selling personal data is prohibited except where the law expressly permits it.

Data leaving Vietnam

Some of our providers store data outside Vietnam. Where personal data of people in Vietnam is transferred abroad, we complete the transfer impact assessment the Law on Personal Data Protection requires, keep it available for inspection, and put contractual protections in place with the recipient.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that go nowhere: deleted within 12 months of our last exchange with you.
  • Reservation and pilot records: kept while your reservation is live, and for 24 months after it ends or you withdraw, so we can evidence what was agreed.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as accounting and tax records: for the period the relevant law sets.
  • Server logs: normally 90 days.

Your rights

Under Vietnamese data protection law you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold and how we are using it;
  • give you a copy of it;
  • correct it if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or stop a particular use of it;
  • withdraw a consent you gave us, which does not undo processing already carried out lawfully;
  • object to processing, and to complain.

Send any of these to ride@nuenmoto.com. We will acknowledge a request to withdraw consent, restrict, or object within 2 working days and act on it within 15 days, extended to 20 days where a third-party processor is involved. Requests to see, correct, or receive a copy of your data are completed within the period the Decree sets, and we will tell you the expected date when we acknowledge.

We may need to confirm who you are before acting, so that we do not disclose one person's data to another.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over an encrypted connection. Access to enquiry records is limited to the people at NUEN MOTO who need it to do their job. We keep the number of systems holding personal data small on purpose.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify the competent authority and affected individuals as the Law on Personal Data Protection requires.

Children

This site is aimed at adults, and at businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. Processing a child's personal data requires verified parental consent under Vietnamese law. If you believe a child has given us their details, tell us and we will delete them.

If you are not satisfied

Come to us first at ride@nuenmoto.com and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the competent Vietnamese authority for personal data protection, which sits under the Ministry of Public Security.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Where a change materially affects you and we hold your contact details, we will tell you directly rather than relying on you to re-read this page.
