Built here, for the people who ride here

We are a Vietnamese company building for Vietnamese roads. The riders who tell us what is wrong with the bike are the reason it gets better.

Why it matters

Give back where we build

Every part of this company sits in Ho Chi Minh City: the workshop, the suppliers, the riders who test for us. Giving something back is not a campaign, it is the same set of relationships that lets us build at all.

01

Ride with us

Group rides out of Ho Chi Minh City, open to anyone on two wheels. You do not need to own one of ours to come along.

02

Rider feedback sessions

Working riders sit with the engineering team and say what is wrong. Several things on the U1 exist because of these sessions.

03

Workshop days

Small groups come to see the frames, the printed parts and the bikes mid build. Students and mechanics get first call.

04

Local suppliers first

Parts are specified against processes Vietnamese suppliers already run. Money spent on the bill of materials stays in the country wherever it can.

05

Training and skills

Electric drivetrains are new to most workshops here. We share what we learn about servicing them, because a bike nobody can fix is worthless.

06

Cleaner streets

A delivery rider covers more kilometers in a week than most people do in a month. Changing what those riders ride is where the air quality math actually moves.
