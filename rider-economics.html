Before You Switch

The real checklist.

Your daily distance

Under about 50 km a day, the fuel you save may not cover the flat subscription. This product is built for working riders, and the more you ride, the more it pays.

Swap coverage on your routes

The economics assume a cabinet inside your working area. Check coverage for your district before committing, and remember the U1 can also charge from a household outlet at roughly 2.5 hours per pack.

Resale value

The second-hand market for electric bikes in Vietnam is young. Petrol bikes hold value predictably today; treat EV resale as unproven for now.

Subscription terms

₫1,450,000 a month reflects current partner pricing. Like any service, terms can change at renewal. The contract, not the brochure, governs.
