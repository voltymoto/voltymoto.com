Fleet questions

What operators ask first.

How is fleet pricing set?
₫30M to ₫35M per unit is an estimate for the first production batch, including 10% VAT, not a confirmed price. The range assumes an order of 100 units or more; smaller fleet orders are quoted individually. Confirmed fleet pricing is set per agreement once the build cost is locked and ordering opens, and depends on volume and configuration.
Who services a fleet, and how often?
We are setting up full servicing with several maintenance partners, and a proper service is done at our own store. Wear items are deliberately ordinary, chain drive, twin rear shocks, a conventional swingarm and disc brakes, so any competent mechanic can handle a roadside fix without waiting for us. Plan on two to three service stops a year per unit, against four to six on petrol. Once warranty terms are confirmed, covered work is on us.
What does the battery cost to run?
The packs are rented rather than bought with the vehicle. Current partner pricing on the unlimited-distance self-charge plans is ₫918,000 a month for three packs (TP3U) or ₫1,188,000 for four (TP4U), VAT included. Swapping at a station is billed separately at ₫8,000 per battery, and charging a pack at home costs roughly ₫6,600 in electricity. A refundable ₫400,000 deposit applies per battery. Battery wear is the network operator’s problem, not yours, and plan terms can change at renewal.
What about software, riding modes and controls?
Most of that we expect to handle remotely. Software, riding modes, throttle and control mapping are updated over the internet rather than by a workshop visit, so a fix or an improvement reaches your bike wherever it is parked. Anything mechanical still needs hands on the bike. The update path is being finalised alongside type approval and we will publish exactly what can be done remotely before delivery.
Can the bikes carry our livery?
Yes. The flat side panel takes an operator livery as a wrap, and boxes bolt to standard mounting points, so every bike in the yard can be the same bike.
I run a fleet. Is the process different?
Yes. Fleet enquiries go to the same form, but tell us your fleet size and districts and we route you to a pilot conversation instead of the retail queue. A pilot is 10 to 100 units on your own routes with your own riders, and an operating report at the end of it.
When can I actually get one?
The U1 is a running prototype in ride testing. No vehicle can be delivered to a customer until whole-vehicle type approval is granted by the Vietnam Register, and we will not publish a delivery date we cannot hold. Everyone on the list hears the date at the same time, before it is announced publicly.
