Three-year horizon

Over a full three-year asset life, one unit costs ₫193.2M to run on petrol and ₫83.7M on the U1. That is ₫109.5M per vehicle, roughly 4,200 USD, recurring for every unit and every replacement cycle the fleet runs.

Assumptions And Exclusions

What this model does and does not include.

Financing excluded

On both sides. Leasing or loan structures change cash flow timing, not the underlying cost gap.

EV incentives excluded

Where registration fee exemptions apply they improve the Volty case further; we keep the comparison conservative.

Residuals are estimates

The used market for electric two-wheelers is young; depreciation lines will firm up with fleet data.

Subscription is contractual

Battery subscription pricing reflects current partner terms per agreement, not a permanent market price.

Insurance held equal

Across both vehicles, and downtime is valued at ₫400,000 per offline day of lost delivery capacity.

Unit price is not set

Planning uses an indicative ₫35,000,000 to ₫45,000,000 range for the first production batch. The MSRP is published when the build cost is locked and reservations open. Fleet pricing is set per agreement.

Rider Economics

Modeled from published U1 specifications and current partner pricing. Basis unless changed above: 200 km per working day, 26 working days per month, RON92 at ₫22,480 per liter, the Selex TP3U unlimited-distance battery plan at ₫918,000 per month with VAT, station swaps billed at ₫8,000 per battery, home charging at about ₫6,600 per pack, a real loaded range of 75 km per pack, and Payback is modeled against the ₫35,000,000 to ₫45,000,000 indicative range for the first production batch. That range is not a confirmed price: the MSRP is published when the build cost is locked and reservations open. Subscription pricing reflects current partner terms and is contractual, not a permanent market price. Real results vary with routes, load, weather, and how you ride. This is a planning tool, not a warranty.
