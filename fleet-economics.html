Three-year horizon

Over a full three-year asset life, one unit costs ₫193.2M to run on petrol and ₫83.7M on the U1. That is ₫109.5M per vehicle, roughly 4,200 USD, recurring for every unit and every replacement cycle the fleet runs.

Assumptions And Exclusions

What this model does and does not include.

Financing excluded

On both sides. Leasing or loan structures change cash flow timing, not the underlying cost gap.

EV incentives excluded

Where registration fee exemptions apply they improve the Volty case further; we keep the comparison conservative.

Residuals are estimates

The used market for electric two-wheelers is young; depreciation lines will firm up with fleet data.

Subscription is contractual

Battery subscription pricing reflects current partner terms per agreement, not a permanent market price.

Insurance held equal

Across both vehicles, and downtime is valued at ₫400,000 per offline day of lost delivery capacity.

Unit price is indicative

₫34,190,000 reflects indicative launch pricing; final pricing is set per fleet agreement.
