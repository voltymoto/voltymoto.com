Rider questions

What riders ask first.

How far does it actually go on a charge?
Up to 195 km on two packs. Fully loaded, in real traffic, plan on 140 to 160 km. A single pack halves that and suits dense inner-city work. Real range moves with speed, load, road, and weather, like any vehicle.
Can I charge at home instead of swapping?
Yes. Take a pack out and put it in a floor dock plugged into a standard household outlet. Roughly 2.5 hours per pack, no wallbox and no installation. Most working riders will do both: swap on route, charge overnight.
Can it carry a passenger?
Yes, with the rear seat fitted. The U1 carries two people once the passenger seat is installed, and the rear deck comes off to make room for it. Run it as a cargo bike through the week and put the seat back on at the weekend. A working bike that cannot carry someone on a Sunday is a bike a rider replaces as soon as they can afford to.
What licence do I need to ride it?
The U1 tops out at 80 km/h, which places it in a licensed motorcycle class rather than the moped category. Final classification is confirmed at type approval, and we will publish it then. Check your own licence class against the approved figures before you buy.
Who services it, and how often?
Bring it to us. We are setting up full servicing with several maintenance partners, and a proper service is done at our own store where the bike can be checked proper end to end. The day-to-day wear items are deliberately ordinary, though: chain drive, twin rear shocks, a conventional swingarm and disc brakes, so any competent mechanic can handle a roadside fix or a tire without waiting for us. Expect two to three service stops a year, brakes and tires, against four to six on a petrol bike. Once warranty terms are confirmed, work covered by the warranty is on us, which is the other reason to bring it in rather than to a corner shop.
What about software, riding modes and controls?
Most of that we expect to handle remotely. Software, riding modes, throttle and control mapping are updated over the internet rather than by a workshop visit, so a fix or an improvement reaches your bike wherever it is parked. Anything mechanical still needs hands on the bike. The update path is being finalised alongside type approval and we will publish exactly what can be done remotely before delivery.
Is there a warranty?
Warranty terms are being finalized alongside type approval and will be published in full before any customer delivery, not after. You will see them before you are asked to pay anything. Our expectation is that covered work is carried out at our store at no cost to you, which is why a proper service belongs with us rather than a corner shop.
