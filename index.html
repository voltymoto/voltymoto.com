Why Now

Adapt or exit.

A working rider covers in a week what a private owner covers in a month. Vietnam is regulating that rider first: the emission roadmap is signed, and Hanoi has already named app-based riders as the group that loses zone access first.

Emission Inspection Becomes Law

  • Signed Apr 2, 2026: Decision 13/2026/QD-TTg sets the national roadmap for motorcycle emission inspection, tied to standard QCVN 99:2025/BNNMT.
  • Jul 1, 2027: inspection starts in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Limits are set by the year a bike was built, so older bikes are tested hardest.
  • Jul 1, 2028: the remaining centrally governed cities. Jul 1, 2030: all other provinces, and provinces may start earlier.
  • For a fleet: every gasoline bike becomes a recurring test, a queue, and a pass or fail. An electric bike has no tailpipe to test.

Hanoi Names Commercial Riders First

  • In force now: Resolution 57/2025/NQ-HDND bans fossil fuel motorcycles working on ride-hailing and delivery apps from the low-emission zone. Private bikes face time and area limits instead. Commercial riders are named separately, and named first.
  • Jul 1, 2026: the zone opened under Decision 3273/QD-UBND, starting with controlled areas in Hoan Kiem by vehicle type and time window rather than a blanket Ring Road 1 ban.
  • 2027 to 2028: scope and emission standards tighten each year. From Jan 1, 2028 the zone covers all of Ring Road 1 and part of Ring Road 2.
  • Before 2030: commercial transport motorcycles are to complete the switch to clean energy.

Ho Chi Minh City, Still A Proposal

  • Status: the HIDS scheme to move roughly 400,000 app drivers and couriers from gasoline to electric has not been enacted. Treat every date in it as a plan, not a rule.
  • As drafted: conversion targets of 30% by end 2026, 80% during 2027, and 100% by the end of the decade, plus an end to new platform registrations for gasoline riders.
  • What is real today: the July 2027 inspection date above, which applies to this city whether or not the conversion scheme passes.
The government is regulating you first.
