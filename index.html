The machine

Built for work

A flat, modular utility deck. A swappable battery bay. One chassis that takes a front rack, a rear rack, or side frames, depending on what the job needs that week.

Estimated cost of the U1, and cost per working day

Two numbers decide whether an electric bike is worth it. What it costs to buy, and what it costs to run for a day of work. The first we can now put a range on. The second depends entirely on your route, so we let you run it yourself rather than quote you an average.

Run it against your own day

Cost per working day is energy, maintenance, depreciation and insurance, set against the petrol bike being ridden today. It moves with distance, load and shift pattern, which is why an average helps nobody. Both calculators start from your own numbers.

Rider economics → You own the bike and ride it yourself. Fuel saved per day, what a pack costs to charge, and how long the switch takes to pay back. Fleet economics → You run vehicles other people ride. Cost per working day across the fleet, uptime against a charging cycle, and what downtime is costing you now.
Audience

Who it is for

Fleet operators are who we build for first. Riders who buy their own bike get the same machine. In both cases the vehicle stays our responsibility, not the swap network's.

01

Ride hailing

High daily kilometers on thin margins per trip. A swap under two minutes keeps the bike earning where a charge stops it.

02

Last mile delivery

Stop and start duty, loaded racks, riders handing over between shifts. 200 kg of payload and mounts front, mid and rear.

03

B2B fleets

Emission inspection becomes mandatory in July 2027 and Hanoi is already piloting a low emission zone. The dates are public.

04

Multi fleet riders

You ride for whoever pays best this hour. The bike is yours, not the fleet's, and it swaps on whichever network is nearest.

05

Commuters

Two trips a day, plus errands and the school run. Run one pack or two, and carry a pack inside to charge, not the bike.

06

Adventure riders

Weekend distance outside the city, gear on the rack. 195 km maximum range, 140 to 160 km loaded, swap where you find one.
