The U1 / Electric Utility Motorcycle

A work motorcycle, not a converted commuter

Drawn around a working day: 200 kg of payload, packs a rider can lift out and swap in under two minutes, and mounting points that let the load sit where the job needs it.

The idea

What a work motorcycle means

Most electric motorcycles sold to riders in Vietnam are commuter bikes with a rack bolted on afterwards. They are designed for one person going to an office and back, then asked to carry a hundred kilos of somebody else's parcels for ten hours a day.

The U1 starts from the opposite end. The frame, the rack geometry and the battery layout were decided by what a working day demands, and the styling followed. That is the whole difference, and it shows up in the parts a spec sheet does not always list.

  • 01Load is designed in, not added on. Mounting points exist at the front, the middle and the rear from the frame up.
  • 02Downtime is the real cost. Energy comes out by hand, so the bike is back on the road in under two minutes rather than off it for hours.
  • 03A working day is not a lab cycle. We publish the loaded range alongside the maximum, because only one of those two numbers pays a rider.

Energy we can control

Run one 2.2 kWh pack when the route is short and the weight matters, or two when the shift is long. Both come out of the frame by hand, so a pack can go up to an apartment to charge while the bike stays on the street.

A swap takes under two minutes. The U1 is not tied to one swap network, which means the operator keeps the choice of supplier rather than inheriting ours.

Removable packs Swap under 2 min Charge anywhere

Load where the job needs it

200 kg of payload, with mounting points at the front, the middle and the rear. A food bag, a parcel cage and a fuel-style courier box do not want the same position on the bike, so the frame does not force one.

152 Nm at the wheel is there for the loaded pull away from a light, which is the load case a delivery bike sees a few hundred times a day.

200 kg payload Front, mid, rear mounts 152 Nm

Range with and without loads

195 km is the maximum. 140 to 160 km is what two packs return with a load on board and city traffic in the way. We publish both because an operator planning shifts needs the second number, and a specification sheet that only carries the first is not usable.

Top speed is 80 km/h, which is above the legal limit on every road a delivery or ride-hailing rider works on in Ho Chi Minh City.
